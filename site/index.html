How it works

Four stages, from the room to a signed-off record

01

Listen

Records the device microphone at 16 kHz, in the room or beside your call. Keeps a local audio archive if you choose.

record · WAV · silero VAD
02

Transcribe

Speech in any language is transcribed on-device with Whisper, or with Deepgram Nova-3 in the cloud when you allow it.

sherpa-onnx Whisper turbo · Nova-3
03

Recognize voices

Every new voice gets a temporary name such as "Speaker 4" and a voiceprint. Rename once and every past and future meeting updates.

speaker embeddings · on-device clustering
04

Write minutes

Claude turns the multilingual transcript into formal Arabic minutes: summary, topics, decisions, owners and deadlines. Export as PDF or Markdown.

Claude Opus · JSON schema · PDF

Accuracy reality check

Arabic speech recognition is still hard, including for us

No meeting notetaker we reviewed publishes an Arabic word error rate, and Raqim does not have one yet either. The figures here are what engine vendors and papers report. They come from different test sets and scoring methods, so read each group on its own.

The engine Raqim runs on-device, Whisper large-v3, sits behind Arabic-specialist models on the public leaderboard and degrades sharply on dialects. That is our biggest technical risk, and the reason a published benchmark is our first launch requirement.

All numbers below are reported, not independently verified. Lower is better. Diarization reference: open pyannote 3.1 reports 22.7% DER on far-field AMI meetings.
